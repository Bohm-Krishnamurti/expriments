About

A nonprofit R&D lab for the incentives behind AI.

Ensuring AI and future economic systems are built and deployed with genuine human objectives at their core.

Origins

AOI was founded by the late internet pioneer Dr. Peter Eckersley, who co-founded the Institute and previously served as Chief Computer Scientist at the Electronic Frontier Foundation and first Director of Research at the Partnership on AI.

The Institute is a non-profit research lab working to ensure that both AI and future economic systems are built and deployed with genuine human objectives at their core, enabled by broad public input, free markets and scalable cooperation.

The organization collaborates with academics, scientists, economists, entrepreneurs, and government and civil society advisors to explore solutions addressing how AI impacts individuals, collectives, and societal systems.

AOI operates as a nonprofit with theoretical research and application development departments, pursuing both internal projects and external collaborations with aligned experts.
