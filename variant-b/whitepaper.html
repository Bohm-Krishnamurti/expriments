A Research Whitepaper

Beneficial deployment of transformative technologies.

AI alignment challenges mirror existing problems in economic regulation and institutional design. Misalignment reflects ongoing tensions between institutional objectives and human flourishing.

Download PDF →

Abstract

Proposes continuity between AI alignment and economic/institutional challenges. Suggests bidirectional knowledge transfer: AI safety can learn from institutional regulation successes, while AI breakthroughs can improve governance and institutions.

Frames AI alignment within institutional context. Notes that misalignment may lead to power concentration rather than catastrophic failure. Emphasizes that institutions already struggle with alignment — corporations, political parties — making AI challenges extensions of existing problems.

Three levels are addressed: inter-institutional (markets, governments), institutional-member (cooperation), and individual (epistemic security).
