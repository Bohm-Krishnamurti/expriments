About

A nonprofit R&D lab for the
incentives behind AI.

AI Objectives Institute (AOI) focuses on harnessing the potential of large-scale systems — AI, markets, and bureaucracies — to advance humanity's interests.

Mission

Ensuring AI and future economic systems are built and deployed with genuine human objectives at their core, enabled by broad public input, free markets and scalable cooperation.

Origin

Founded by Peter Eckersley

The late internet pioneer who co-founded the Institute. Previously Chief Computer Scientist at EFF and first Director of Research at the Partnership on AI.

Approach

Working groups

We collaborate with and employ working groups of academics, scientists, economists, entrepreneurs, and government officials.

Structure

Theory and application

AOI operates with theoretical research and application development departments, pursuing both internal projects and external collaborations.

Funders

SFF · FLI · Plurality · Donors

Survival and Flourishing Foundation, Future of Life Institute, Plurality, and individual donors.
