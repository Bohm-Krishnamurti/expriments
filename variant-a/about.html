About

A nonprofit R&D lab for the incentives behind AI.

AI Objectives Institute (AOI) focuses on harnessing the potential of large-scale systems — AI, markets, and bureaucracies — to advance humanity's interests.

Mission

Ensuring AI and future economic systems are built and deployed with genuine human objectives at their core.

Founded by the late internet pioneer Dr. Peter Eckersley, AOI works to ensure that both AI and future economic systems are built and deployed with genuine human objectives at their core, enabled by broad public input, free markets and scalable cooperation.

We collaborate with and employ working groups of academics, scientists, economists, entrepreneurs, and government officials. Our team builds tools and develops research that facilitates the pursuit of beneficial systemic objectives — at the intersection of democracy, markets and AI progress.
