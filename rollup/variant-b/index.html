An AI-enabled roll-up controls legacy service businesses, then uses AI-native software, centralized ops, and shared data to transform delivery. The right to redesign the work comes from ownership.

Europe Opportunity

More than $4tn across information, professional, and administrative services — many workflows still text- and voice-heavy, and underpenetrated by vertical SaaS. [2]

Clearest wedges

Property & lettings (Dwelly, Buena, Arbio, hallo theo); tax/accounting/payroll (Integral, Taxforce); legal delivery; SME admin services; hospitality/rental ops.

Why underpriced

High local fragmentation; compliance + language complexity creating defensible know-how; low historical vertical-SaaS penetration making software-enabled ownership more powerful than procurement.

The catch

Cross-border scaling is harder than the US. Labor law, tax, licensing, language, and consumer regulation differ by country. Winners may look like country-by-country operating systems, not one pan-European platform.

Signal

Tenet’s Berlin-based AI roll-up fund targets European professional-services roll-ups, betting that low SaaS penetration favors AI-native operators who buy rather than sell. [10]

Holding-Company Strategy

The second-order thesis: a permanent-capital holdco owns multiple operating businesses and applies one shared AI operating system across them.

SINGLE VERTICAL vs HOLDING COMPANY
DimensionSingle-vertical roll-upAI-native holdco
ScopeOne categoryMultiple, shared primitives
CapitalBuy-and-build, vertical financingPermanent / long-horizon
AI assetAutomation in one verticalShared OS, routing, pipelines, playbooks
Data edgeProprietary vertical dataCross-company loops + per-business tuning
Main riskIntegration & executionOverreach; proving the OS generalizes

Broad comparables: Beacon Software (broadest horizontal platform) [23][24] and Long Lake (the $6.3bn Amex GBT transaction). [25][26]

> Bottom line: the most investable AI roll-up is an operating company that acquires the right to redesign work. The winner may be the best AI deployment operator across many businesses — not the best consolidator in one.
