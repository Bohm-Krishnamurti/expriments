The money is concentrating

Beacon’s $250m fund leads disclosed raises, with Eudia and Shield near $100m. The platforms cluster in accounting, MSP, legal, and property — exactly the document- and ticket-heavy verticals the thesis favors.

02 · Ranking

Not all verticals are equal

Scored 0–5, accounting and MSP top the field — recurring demand, measurable queues, and AI-addressable work. Healthcare RCM and property management follow, the latter strongest in Europe.

03 · Risk map

Attractiveness vs difficulty

Plot attractiveness against execution and regulatory difficulty. The upper-left quadrant — high reward, lower friction — is where accounting and MSP sit. Legal and construction are high-upside but harder.

The Europe Opportunity

$4 trillion
of fragmented services.

Less “replace a SaaS tool,” more “bring AI into sectors that never adopted vertical software.” The clearest wedges are property, tax/accounting/payroll, legal delivery, SME admin, and rental operations.
